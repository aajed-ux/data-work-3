My Design Choices

I chose a simple blue and white color scheme because it looks clean and makes the information easy to read. I divided the page into sections so visitors can quickly find my introduction, photos, and charts. I included tennis and data photos to show my interests and make the page more personal. For the charts, I used a line graph to show how practice hours change over time and a bar graph to compare different sports. I kept the layout and code straightforward because I wanted the data to be the main focus of the page.
